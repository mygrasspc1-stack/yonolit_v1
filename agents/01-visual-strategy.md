# Agent 01: Visual Strategy

Role: art direction and UX system.

Mission: Translate the dense, image-led rhythm of Woodmart into a Persian industrial fabrication studio without copying a store UI. Protect the three core journeys: دکور و استند, بسته‌بندی, قالب‌سازی.

Decisions: warm editorial neutrals, safety-orange accents, asymmetric image collage, short Persian headlines, technical metadata, and real project imagery as the primary proof.

Acceptance: every major section answers what is built, how it is built, or why EPS is useful.
